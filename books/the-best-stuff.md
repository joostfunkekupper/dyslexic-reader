# The Best Stuff

Mum sent Pip and Tim to the shop.

"Get me some carrots, ten plums, six spuds and a box of mints," she said.

"Here is the list. Go to Big Bill's shop. He sells the best stuff."

Pip and Tim ran up to the hill to the shop. Tim held on to the list.

All of a sudden, a big gust of wind swept the list out of Tim's hand!

He saw it spin up, up, UP and drift down on the tram tracks.

"Oh no! I have lost the list. Mum will be cross," said Tim.

"No stress. We can still do it," said Pip.

Pip and Tim went into Big Bill's shop and he put all the stuff into a big box.

Back home, Mum said, "Did you get me some carrots?"

"Uhmm, we got you a parrot," said Tim.

"Did you get ten plums?" asked Mum.

"We got ten plugs," said Pip.

"Did you get me six spuds and a box of mints?" asked Mum.

"We got you six slugs and a box of pins," said Pip.

Was Mum cross? No, she just had a grin.

"Next time, we will have to stick the list on with a pin!" she laughed.
