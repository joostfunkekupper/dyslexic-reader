# The Knight and the Sneezing Dragon
<!-- sticker: 🐉 -->

Sir Max is a young knight. He has a shiny helmet, a wooden shield and a brave horse called Clover.

One morning, the people of the village run to the castle.

"Help!" they shout. "There is a dragon on the hill. Every time it roars, fire shoots out and burns our fields!"

Sir Max puts on his helmet. He climbs onto Clover. "Do not worry," he says. "I will go and see."

Up the hill they ride. Clip, clop, clip, clop.

At the top, Sir Max sees the dragon. It is big and green, with wings like sails.

The dragon opens its mouth. "Ah... ah... AH-CHOO!"

A puff of fire flies out. It lands on the grass. Sizzle!

Sir Max lifts his shield. Then he stops. The dragon is not roaring. It is sneezing!

"Are you sick?" asks Sir Max.

The dragon sniffs. "My nose is so itchy," it says in a small voice. "It is all these flowers. I cannot stop sneezing."

Sir Max looks around. The hill is covered in yellow flowers.

"I know a place with no flowers," he says. "Follow me."

Sir Max and Clover lead the dragon to a rocky cave by the sea. The air is cool and salty.

The dragon takes a big breath. No sneeze! It smiles a wide, toothy smile.

"Thank you, Sir Max," it says. "My name is Ember. Can we be friends?"

From then on, the fields are safe. And on cold nights, Ember warms up the whole village with one gentle puff.
