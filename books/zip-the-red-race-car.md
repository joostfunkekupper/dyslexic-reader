# Zip the Red Race Car

Zip is a red race car.

Zip is fast. Zip can zip and zip!

Zip is at the track.

Bud is a big tan van. Bud is at the track as well.

Bud is not fast. Bud is a bit slow.

Zip and Bud get set.

Get set... Go!

Zip zips off. Bud huffs and puffs.

Zip gets to the bend.

Zip hits a bump! Zip skids in the mud.

Zip is stuck. Zip can not get up the hill.

Bud gets to the mud.

"I can help," said Bud.

Bud has a rope. Bud and Zip tug and tug.

Zip is up! Zip is not stuck!

Zip and Bud go up the hill.

Zip and Bud get to the end, and the kids yell and clap.

"You are the best pal," said Zip.
