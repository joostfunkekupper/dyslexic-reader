# The Lighthouse Cat

On a small island at the edge of the sea, there was a tall white lighthouse. A grey cat named Pebble lived at the very top. Every evening, Pebble watched the keeper climb the long spiral stairs to light the great lamp.

Pebble loved the light. It swept across the water like a slow, golden arm. Boats far away would see it and know where the rocks were hiding.

## The storm

One night, a storm rolled in from the west. The wind howled and the rain hammered on the glass. The keeper climbed the stairs, but the lamp would not light.

"The wick is soaked," the keeper said. "I need a dry one, and fast."

Pebble remembered something. Under the old chair in the kitchen, there was a box the keeper had forgotten about. Pebble raced down all one hundred and twelve steps.

---

The box was still there. Inside, wrapped in a cloth, was a bundle of dry wicks. Pebble grabbed the cloth in her teeth and dragged it back up the stairs, one step at a time.

The keeper laughed when he saw her. "You clever cat!" he said. Soon the lamp was burning bright again.

Far out at sea, a small fishing boat saw the light. It turned away from the rocks and sailed safely home.

From that night on, the keeper always kept a spare wick by the lamp. And Pebble always got the warmest spot by the window.
