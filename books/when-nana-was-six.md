# When Nana Was Six

Ruby is six years old. One day, she asks her nana a question.

"Nana, what was it like when you were six?"

Nana smiles. She gets out an old box of photos.

The photos are not in colour. They are black and white.

Nana points to a photo of a big wooden box. "This was our TV. It only showed black and white pictures too."

Ruby is surprised. Her TV is flat and thin, and it shows every colour.

Next, Nana shows her a phone. It is stuck to the wall, and it has a long curly cord.

"We could not take the phone outside," says Nana. "And it could not take photos!"

Nana shows Ruby a photo of her class. There are lots of children in rows.

"We wrote on little boards with chalk," says Nana. "We did not have computers."

Ruby says, "We use tablets at my school. But we use pencils too."

Nana points to another photo. It is a tram on a busy street.

"I rode the tram to the city with my mum," says Nana.

Ruby claps. "I ride the tram too!"

"What games did you play?" asks Ruby.

"We played hopscotch and marbles," says Nana. "And we played chasey in the park."

Ruby jumps up. "I play chasey too!"

That night, Ruby draws a picture. On one side, she draws Nana's old TV and phone. On the other side, she draws her own TV and tablet.

In the middle, she draws Nana and herself playing chasey.
