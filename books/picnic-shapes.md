# The Picnic of Shapes

Ava and her little brother Leo are having a picnic in the park.

Mum spreads out a big square rug.

Ava looks at the food. She sees lots of shapes.

The plates are circles. The sandwiches are squares. The crackers are rectangles.

Leo holds up a slice of watermelon. "This one is a triangle!" he says.

Ava counts the sides. "1, 2, 3. Yes!"

There is one big sandwich left. Ava and Leo both want it.

Mum says, "Let's cut it into halves."

She cuts the sandwich right down the middle. Now there are two pieces.

Ava gets one half. Leo gets one half. That is fair!

Next, there is a bowl of strawberries. Ava counts them. There are 10 strawberries.

Ava says, "Let's share them into two groups."

She puts one in Leo's bowl, and one in her bowl. She does it again and again.

Now each bowl has 5 strawberries.

After lunch, they go on a shape hunt in the park.

The wheel on the bike is a circle. The sign on the path is a triangle. The window on the toilet block is a rectangle.

Leo finds a leaf. "What shape is this?" he asks.

Ava laughs. "Leaves can be all sorts of shapes. That one looks like a heart!"

It was the best picnic ever. Even the shapes had fun.

---

Can you find a circle, a square, a triangle and a rectangle in your room?

If you cut an apple into halves, how many pieces do you get?

How could you share 8 grapes fairly between two people?
