# Four Seasons in One Day

In the morning, it is cold. Sam can see his breath in the air.

He puts on a jumper and a beanie. It feels like winter.

Then the clouds roll in. Drip, drop, drip. It starts to rain.

Sam puts on his raincoat and boots. He jumps in a puddle. Splash!

At lunch time, the rain stops. The sun comes out. Now it is hot!

Sam takes off his jumper. It feels like summer.

Mum says, "Time to be SunSmart."

Sam slips on a shirt. He slops on sunscreen. He slaps on a hat. He seeks some shade. He slides on his sunglasses.

In the afternoon, the wind starts to blow. Whoosh!

Leaves blow off the trees. They twirl and swirl in the air. It feels like autumn.

At night, Sam looks out the window. The sky is clear, and he can see the stars.

Sam tells Mum, "Today was cold, rainy, hot and windy!"

Mum laughs. "That is Melbourne. Four seasons in one day!"
