# Shadows and Sounds

It is a rainy day, so Zara and her grandpa are playing inside.

Grandpa turns off the big light. He switches on a torch.

Grandpa shines the torch at the wall. It makes a bright circle of light.

Zara puts her hand in front of the torch. A dark shape appears on the wall.

"A shadow!" says Grandpa.

Zara makes her hand into a bird. The shadow bird flaps its wings on the wall.

She moves her hand closer to the torch. The shadow gets bigger! She moves her hand away from the torch. The shadow gets smaller.

Zara holds up a clear plastic cup. Its shadow is so pale that she can hardly see it.

Next, Grandpa gets out a drum.

Zara taps the drum softly. Tap, tap, tap.

She hits the drum hard. Boom, boom, boom!

Grandpa puts some rice on top of the drum. When Zara hits the drum, the rice jumps and dances.

"Look, the drum is shaking!" says Grandpa.

Zara puts her hand on her throat. She hums a song. She can feel her throat buzzing!

Zara laughs. "My throat is shaking like the drum!"

After dinner, Zara puts on a show for the family.

She makes shadow animals on the wall. Grandpa plays the drum. Boom for the elephant. Tap, tap for the little mouse.

Everyone claps. Clap, clap, clap!
