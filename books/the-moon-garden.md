# The Moon Garden

Ada's grandmother had a garden that only bloomed at night. In the daytime it looked plain, just green leaves and closed buds. But when the moon came up, everything changed.

The flowers opened slowly, one by one. They were white and silver and pale blue. Some of them glowed softly, like tiny lanterns.

"Why do they wait for the dark?" Ada asked.

Her grandmother smiled. "Because the moths come at night. The flowers want to be found by them."

## A visitor

That evening, Ada sat very still on the garden bench. She waited and waited. Her eyes grew heavy.

Then something brushed past her cheek. It was a moth, big and soft, with wings like brown velvet. It drifted from flower to flower, taking a sip from each one.

Ada held her breath. More moths came, and then more. The whole garden seemed to be dancing.

## Morning

When the sun came up, the flowers closed again. The moths were gone. Ada's grandmother was already in the kitchen making toast.

"Did you see them?" her grandmother asked.

"I saw them," Ada said. "Can I plant my own moon garden?"

Her grandmother handed her a small paper bag of seeds. *"I was hoping you would ask."*
