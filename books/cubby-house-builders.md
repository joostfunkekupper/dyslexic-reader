# The Cubby House Builders

Mia and Jack want to build a cubby house for Teddy. Dark clouds are coming.

First, they try paper. They fold it into a little roof.

Jack pours a cup of water on top. Oh no! The paper gets soggy, and it tears. Water drips onto Teddy.

Mia says, "Poor Teddy!"

Next, they try a woolly blanket. It is soft and warm.

Jack pours more water on top. The blanket gets wet and heavy. Teddy gets wet again.

Jack says, "Soggy again!"

Then they try a plastic sheet from the shed.

Jack pours the water on top. The water runs off the plastic, and it drips onto the grass.

Teddy stays dry! Mia claps her hands. "It works!"

Last of all, Dad helps them use some old pieces of wood.

They make walls with the wood. They put the plastic sheet on top for the roof.

They put the soft woolly blanket inside for a bed.

That night, it rains. In the morning, they run outside.

Teddy is warm and dry. He looks very happy in his new home.
