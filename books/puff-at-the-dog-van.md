# Puff at the Dog Van

I am Tess.

Puff is my dog.

Puff is in a bit of a mess!

I will get him to the dog van.

Jill is the boss of the dog van.

She tells us to hop in.

The van is set up so well for dogs!

Jill gets her mitts and rubs the big bits of mud off.

She fills the tub.

Puff has a dip and a rub.

Jill tells Puff to sit for the hot jet.

In the din, I yell, "Do not fuss, Puff! It will not kill you!"

Ruff!

Jill gives Puff a cut.

She cuts off a lot of fuzz!

Yes! Puff is in less of a mess!

He looks fab.

But Jill's van is in a big mess!

"I will get the vac!" she says.
