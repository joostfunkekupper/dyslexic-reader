# Robot Rosie Follows the Steps

Isla has a toy robot called Rosie. Rosie is small and round, with big blue eyes.

Isla wants Rosie to go to the toy box.

Isla says, "Rosie, go forward one step."

Rosie rolls forward one step. Beep!

"Now turn right," says Isla. Rosie turns right.

"Go forward two steps." Rosie rolls forward. One, two. Beep, beep!

Rosie is at the toy box. Isla cheers. "You did it!"

Next, Isla wants Rosie to go to the door.

Isla says, "Go forward three steps. Turn left. Go forward one step."

Rosie rolls forward and turns. Then she bumps into the couch! Bonk!

Isla laughs. "Oops! There is a bug in my steps."

Isla looks at the room again. She needed to turn right, not left.

Isla fixes the steps. "Go forward three steps. Turn right. Go forward one step."

This time, Rosie gets to the door.

Isla thinks. "I follow steps every day too!"

To brush her teeth, she gets her toothbrush. She puts on toothpaste. She brushes all her teeth. Then she rinses her mouth.

Before bed, Isla makes a dance for Rosie.

Spin around. Go forward. Go back. Spin around again.

Rosie dances across the floor. Beep, beep, boop!

Isla smiles. "Good night, Rosie. Tomorrow we will make new steps."
