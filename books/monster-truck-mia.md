# Monster Truck Mia

Mia has a big blue monster truck called Thunder. Its wheels are taller than Mia!

Today is the monster truck show. Mia puts on her helmet and climbs into Thunder.

Vroom, vroom! The engine roars. The crowd cheers.

First, Mia drives over a row of old cars. Crunch, crunch, crunch! Thunder bounces up and down.

Next comes the big ramp. Mia takes a deep breath.

She drives faster and faster. Up the ramp she goes, and Thunder flies into the air!

For a moment, everything is quiet. Then... THUMP! Thunder lands on all four wheels.

The crowd jumps to their feet. "Mia! Mia! Mia!"

Then Mia sees something. A little truck called Pebble is stuck in a deep mud pit. Its wheels spin and spin, but it cannot get out.

Mia drives Thunder to the edge of the pit. She ties a strong rope to Pebble.

"Hold on!" she calls.

Thunder pulls. The mud squelches. Slowly, slowly, Pebble rolls out of the pit.

Pebble's driver waves. "Thank you, Mia!"

At the end of the show, Mia gets a shiny gold cup. It is not for the biggest jump.

It says, "Kindest Driver."

Mia holds it up high. That is the best prize of all.
