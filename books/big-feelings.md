# Big Feelings

On Monday, Felix plays soccer with his friends. He kicks a goal!

He feels happy. His face has a big smile, and he wants to jump up and down.

On Tuesday, Felix's friend Tom is away sick. Felix has no one to sit with at lunch.

He feels sad. His eyes feel wet, and his body feels heavy.

Felix tells his teacher. She helps him find someone new to play with. Felix feels a bit better.

On Wednesday, Felix's little sister knocks over his block tower.

He feels angry! His face feels hot, and his hands go tight.

Felix wants to yell. Instead, he stops. He takes a big, slow breath in. Then he lets a big, slow breath out.

He does it three times. His body feels calmer. Then he says, "Please be careful of my tower."

On Thursday, Felix has to read to the whole class. He feels worried.

His tummy feels like it has butterflies inside.

Mum says, "Let's practise together."

Felix practises reading at home. When he reads to the class, he still feels a little worried. But he does it anyway!

On Friday, Felix gets a sticker for trying his best. He feels proud.

He stands up tall, and he cannot stop smiling.

---

How does your body feel when you are happy? What about when you are angry?

Who can you talk to when you feel sad or worried?

Try taking three big, slow breaths. How do you feel now?
