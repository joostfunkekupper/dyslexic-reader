# Who Lives in the Garden?

Noah has a magnifying glass. Today he is a garden explorer.

He kneels down in the backyard and looks very closely.

First, Noah finds a worm in the soil. The worm is long and pink.

It has no legs and no eyes. It wiggles back down into the dark soil.

Next, Noah finds a snail on a leaf. The snail carries a shell on its back.

When Noah touches the leaf, the snail hides in its shell.

A magpie hops across the grass. It is black and white.

The magpie has a strong beak. It pokes the beak into the grass and pulls out a worm.

Noah says, "Oh no, poor worm!"

Mum says, "Magpies get hungry too."

Under a rock, Noah spots a fat lizard with a short tail.

Mum whispers, "That is a blue-tongue lizard."

The lizard opens its mouth. Its tongue really is blue!

The lizard looks at the snail on the leaf. Noah moves the snail up high, just in case.

That night, Noah draws a picture of the garden. He draws the worm, the snail, the magpie and the lizard.

He makes sure there is a home for everyone.
