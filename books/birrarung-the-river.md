# Birrarung, the River

Today, Ms Tran's class is going on a walk to the river.

Everyone wears a hat. Everyone holds a buddy's hand.

At the river, the class meets Aunty May. Aunty May is a Wurundjeri Elder.

Aunty May says, "Welcome to Wurundjeri Country."

Aunty May points to the water. "Many people call this river the Yarra. Its Wurundjeri name is Birrarung."

The children say the name together. "Birrarung."

Aunty May asks the children to be very quiet. "Close your eyes and listen."

Kai hears the water rushing past. Lily hears a bird singing in a tall tree. Omar hears the wind in the leaves.

The class opens their eyes. They see tall gum trees and green grass. They see a duck swimming with her ducklings.

Then Kai spots a chip packet on the ground. Lily sees a plastic bottle near the water.

The class picks up the rubbish and puts it in a bag. Ms Tran makes sure they all wash their hands afterwards.

Aunty May smiles. "Thank you for caring for Country."

Back at school, the class makes a big picture of the river.

They draw the gum trees, the ducks and the water. At the top, they write a word in big letters.

Birrarung.
